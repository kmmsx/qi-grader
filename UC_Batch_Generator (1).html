Step 1 — Export your SharePoint list to CSV
Each week you'll export the California UC Reporting Data Tracking list from SharePoint. Here's how:
1
Open your SharePoint site and navigate to the California UC Reporting Data Tracking list
2
Click Export → Export to Excel in the top toolbar. This downloads an .iqy file — open it and it will load in Excel
3
In Excel, click File → Save As, change the format to CSV UTF-8 (Comma delimited), and save it somewhere easy to find like your Desktop
4
Come back here and either drag that CSV file onto the box below, or click to browse for it
